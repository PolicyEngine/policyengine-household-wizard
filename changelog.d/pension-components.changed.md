`toV1HouseholdPayload` now sends `pensionIncome`, a pension of unknown source, as `taxable_private_pension_income` instead of the `taxable_pension_income` total. The total left both components at zero, so private pension exclusions never applied. For callers that keep using `pensionIncome`, results change in Missouri on PolicyEngine US 1.764.6, and in Alabama, Louisiana, Missouri and New York on 2.15.16 (single-filer profiles tested). The adapter also skips income amounts that are not finite numbers, so a `NaN` in one field cannot erase another field that shares its variable.
