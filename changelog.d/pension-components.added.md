Add `publicPensionIncome` and `privatePensionIncome` to `USPersonIncomes`, sent as `taxable_public_pension_income` and `taxable_private_pension_income`. State rules in PolicyEngine US read those components. Collecting `publicPensionIncome` lets government-pension exemptions apply: on PolicyEngine US 1.764.6 (the live API) those are in Arizona, Idaho, Minnesota, Missouri and Nebraska, and 2.15.16 adds Alabama, Kansas, Louisiana, Massachusetts and New York. `normalizeLegacyDraft` reads the new fields and per-person `pension_income_annual`, `public_pension_income_annual` and `private_pension_income_annual`.
