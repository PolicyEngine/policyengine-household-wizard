`ssiAmount` is sent as `ssi` only when every person in the draft has an amount (0 included); otherwise the model computes everyone's SSI, and `validate()` reports `person.ssiAmount.requiredWhenAnyReceives` for each person without one. An entered 0 is always sent, as `takes_up_ssi_if_eligible: false`, which affects that person only. `isFullTimeStudent` is sent as `is_full_time_college_student` instead of `is_full_time_student`, and only from age 18: PolicyEngine US counts everyone aged 5 to 17 as a K-12 student by age. Rules for college students (for example SNAP's student rule, New Jersey's exemption for dependents attending college and Colorado's EITC student rule) now see flagged people aged 18 and over; flagged 18- and 19-year-old high school students are treated as college students.
